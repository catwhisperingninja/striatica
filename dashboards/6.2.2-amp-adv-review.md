## 1. Scientific red team

### 1.1 Participation ratio is not supported by the cited Geometric Wall paper

The draft says PR is “the field-standard ID estimator per Geometric Wall `2605.09887`.” The paper says otherwise:

- [The Geometric Wall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09887) studies **activation manifolds** across model layers.
- Its Appendix B identifies **TWO-NN** as the intrinsic-dimension estimator.
- It averages per-point estimates in local neighborhoods of `K=100`, then over a layer.
- It does not use participation ratio as its intrinsic-dimension estimator and does not estimate dimension on decoder-weight neighborhoods.

Striatica’s PR implementation may exactly compute covariance effective rank,

\[
d_{\mathrm{PR}} = \frac{(\operatorname{tr} C)^2}{\operatorname{tr}(C^2)},
\]

but exact evaluation of that formula does **not** make it an exact manifold intrinsic dimension. It is a spectral effective-rank statistic whose interpretation depends on neighborhood construction, anisotropy, noise, and sample size.

**Required replacement:**

- Call the current output `participation-ratio effective rank` until its scientific contract is settled.
- Do not use a citation to select a “referee.” Define the target estimand first.
- Treat exact TWO-NN, PR, and local density as separate methods with separate assumptions.
- Verify whether the repo’s existing “TwoNN-inspired” path matches the cited algorithm before attaching the paper’s name to it.
- Pre-register calibration on known-dimension benchmarks and real-derived decoder neighborhoods before renewing paper claims.

### 1.2 Bhalla supports the SAE control arm, not the transcoder conclusion

[Bhalla et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.28119), *Do Sparse Autoencoders Capture Concept Manifolds?*, provides strong evidence that SAEs can tile manifolds with fragmented, partially redundant feature groups (“dilution”). It evaluates multiple SAE architectures, synthetic manifolds, and Llama activation/SAE-code data.

It does **not** study transcoders. Therefore this draft statement is not attributable to Bhalla:

> transcoder geometry clean where SAEs fragment, per Bhalla

The defensible formulation is:

> Bhalla motivates the SAE fragmentation control. Whether matched transcoders exhibit cleaner decoder geometry is Striatica’s preregistered comparative hypothesis.

This distinction matters because a null result—PR and density disagreeing, or transcoders fragmenting too—must remain publishable rather than being defined away as a failed implementation.

Bhalla is also closer competitive overlap than the draft implies: its Ising/conditional-dependence grouping is designed to discount broadly active features and indirect co-activation. That is directly relevant to Striatica’s known Jaccard contamination problem, even though the paper does not extend the method to transcoders or attribution graphs.

### 1.3 `2509.02565` is not theory-only

[Michaud, Gorton, and McGrath](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02565), *Understanding sparse autoencoder scaling in the presence of feature manifolds*, includes:

- numerical capacity-allocation simulations;
- trained ReLU and JumpReLU SAEs on synthetic manifolds;
- geometry-dependent loss-scaling curves;
- preliminary probes of decoder-neighbor similarity on Gemma Scope and vision SAEs.

The paper calls its real-world conclusion preliminary and does not settle the pathological-scaling question. That supports “framing-heavy/open,” not “theory-only/closed/no scoop risk.”

**Required replacement:** reopen it as an active adjacent work item. The competitive risk is not that it already ships Striatica’s atlas; it is that the Goodfire authors have already framed and empirically probed the same SAE-manifold scaling mechanism and are well placed to scale it up.

### 1.4 Density, dimension, and VGT must remain distinct

The draft still tends to collapse three estimands:

| Quantity | What it measures | What it must not be called |
|---|---|---|
| Participation ratio | Effective spectral rank of a local covariance | Exact/topological intrinsic dimension |
| TWO-NN | Dimension inferred from first/second neighbor-distance ratios under its model assumptions | Local density |
| kNN density/radius | Local sampling concentration | Dimension without a model connecting the two |
| VGT growth curve | Scale-dependent neighborhood-growth diagnostic currently used for the detail chart | A scalar local-dimension estimate without a validated estimator contract |

The killer experiment survives disagreement only if these are recorded independently and the primary endpoint is selected before results are inspected. “PR as referee + density” is not enough because PR and density can disagree for legitimate geometric reasons.

### 1.5 Publication posture

The distinct contribution remains defensible but narrower:

- matched SAE/transcoder **decoder-weight** geometry;
- exact deployed-dictionary identity;
- integration with attribution-graph evidence;
- an inspectable atlas rather than only aggregate regressions.

It is not defensible to call the competitor list empty. Geometric Wall overlaps activation-manifold geometry and cross-layer ID; Bhalla overlaps decoder-atom grouping and manifold fragmentation; Michaud et al. overlap SAE geometry/scaling. Striatica’s differentiator is the matched transcoder experiment and graph linkage, neither of which those papers currently supply.

## 2. Current-code audit: the live defect differs from the draft

### 2.1 What production currently writes

The generation path currently does this:

1. `pipeline/cli.py:614` computes participation ratio into `local_dims`.
2. `pipeline/cli.py:619` computes VGT; the scalar array is discarded as `_`, while growth curves are retained.
3. `pipeline/prepare.py:124-129` writes:
   - `localDimensions = local_dims` — therefore PR;
   - `dimMethod = "pr"`;
   - `growthCurves = growth_curves`.

The wire-format key is already `dimMethod`; the frontend also expects `dimMethod`. Keep camelCase at the JSON boundary. Python variables and arguments may remain snake_case internally.

### 2.2 The claimed live `dimMethod`/`dim_method` gate bypass does not exist on `main`

There is no `local_dim_concordance_gate` or proposed `load_local_dim` on current `main`. The snake/camel mismatch is in PR #8’s markdown remediation snippet, not in landed production code.

The current live defect is more direct:

- `audit/run_audit.py::load_vgt()` reads `data["localDimensions"]`;
- it does not inspect `dimMethod` at all;
- it names the result `vgt` even though current generation stores PR there;
- the audit memo writes VGT-named correlation fields from that mislabeled input.

v6.2.2 should distinguish **live defect** from **defect in a proposed fix**. Otherwise implementation work may “fix” a gate that has not yet been written while leaving the current audit consumer wrong.

### 2.3 Provenance is also silently defaulted in the UI

The provenance contract is not only an audit concern:

- `frontend/src/types/feature.ts` makes `dimMethod` optional and accepts `'pr' | 'twonn' | 'vgt'` by comment only.
- `frontend/src/components/StatusBar.tsx` renders missing provenance as `pr` via `dimMethod ?? 'pr'`.
- Consumers color and sort `localDimensions` without validating method compatibility.

A dataset with dimensions but no provenance is therefore silently certified as PR in the viewer. Required contract:

- `localDimensions` and `dimMethod` are an all-or-none pair;
- missing or unsupported methods fail validation before audit/scientific gates;
- the UI must not infer `pr` from absence;
- method support should be an explicit finite schema, not an unconstrained string.

### 2.4 `L1-DIM-BOUNDS` is useful but cannot establish meaning

The `[0,100]` bound would reject the known scalar VGT maximum of 231.9. It does not prove that an in-range array is dimension, that the estimator is PR, or that values correspond to the same feature ordering.

The bound should be a method-specific plausibility check after:

1. finite numeric validation;
2. feature-count/index alignment;
3. required provenance validation;
4. estimator-specific invariants.

The plan should not generalize `[0,100]` to every future dimension method without justification. Ambient dimension and estimator behavior supply the natural contract; a hard 100 ceiling is dataset/method policy, not mathematics.

### 2.5 Growth-curve validation is weaker than v6.2 states

`validate_level1_arrays()` can accept growth curves and only checks outer length. But the production generation call is:

```python
validate_level1_arrays(coords, labels, local_dims)
```

It does **not pass `growth_curves` at all**. Thus `L1-CURVES` does not run during production generation. The separate JSON validator also checks only outer length and is not invoked by that generation call.

Required curve-content contract should include, at minimum:

- outer feature alignment;
- valid and ordered radii/scales;
- finite numeric counts/statistics;
- per-curve shape agreement;
- method/version metadata;
- a negative fixture showing the validator rejects semantically malformed but correctly sized curves.

### 2.6 Another dormant validation path

`validate_level2()` can calculate `L2-DIM-CORR` between activation frequency and local dimension, but the production caller does not pass `frac_nonzero`. That check therefore does not run in normal generation. Any non-activation-frequency claim must cite the separate analysis that actually executed, not imply the production scorecard establishes it.

### 2.7 Metric migration risk is real but not yet quantified

CodeRabbit correctly found naming consumers:

- Grafana queries `striatica_vgt_mean` and `striatica_vgt_mean_by_cluster`;
- `pipeline.metrics` registers `striatica_vgt_mean`;
- the audit memo writes `vgt_correlation_pearson_r`.

However, no production caller of `set_vgt_stats()` was found; current references are tests and documentation. “Renaming will drop dashboard data” is therefore possible, not established.

Required plan step before choosing alias/dual-write/versioning:

1. establish whether production metrics are emitted and retained anywhere;
2. inventory persisted dashboard/audit consumers;
3. decide whether compatibility is required;
4. if required, time-bound dual-write and define removal; otherwise make one atomic breaking rename.

Do not preserve a misleading metric indefinitely merely because a dashboard JSON mentions it.

### 2.8 One CodeRabbit suggestion is not a live issue

`load_vgt()` already uses a context manager. CodeRabbit’s context-manager comment applies to the markdown’s proposed loader snippets, not current code. Keep it as an implementation quality requirement, not a production bug.

## 3. Replacement SAE branch-off gate

### 3.1 Options A/B/C collapse to one valid gate

- **Option B violates the fixed constraint.** A checkpoint taken before UMAP stabilization is not the requested post-both-migrations checkpoint. A provisional commit already exists naturally in Git history; naming it a staged freeze adds ceremony without assurance.
- **Option C’s same-SHA/same-lock freshness is mandatory**, not optional overhead. Results produced from different code or dependency graphs cannot jointly verify one state.
- **Option A is still insufficient** because it omits shared metric correctness and the vacuous robustness gate.

Replace all three with:

> Branch-off readiness is a strict AND across shared scientific contracts, non-vacuous robustness, transcoder identity/migration, and UMAP reproducibility. All execution evidence must identify one code SHA, one committed lock hash, one canonical CPU image digest, and immutable input hashes.

### 3.2 Gate matrix

| Gate | Pass condition | Durable evidence | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0 — immutable basis | Clean exact SHA; `pyproject.toml` and `poetry.lock` hashes; canonical CPU image digest; Python/platform; all input/fixture hashes recorded. | Machine-readable manifest. | **Fail:** no canonical lock-consuming Docker image. |
| G1 — dimension contract | Canonical `dimMethod`; all-or-none value/provenance schema; live audit and UI consumers reject absent/unsupported provenance; PR/density/VGT names separated. | Positive PR fixture plus negative missing/unsupported/wrong-method fixtures. | **Fail:** audit ignores provenance; UI defaults absence to PR. |
| G2 — content validation | Dimension values and growth curves receive value-level, alignment, finite, and method-specific checks in the production path. | Negative perturbation tests prove each gate fails when content is corrupt. | **Fail:** curves are not passed to production L1 validation. |
| G3 — robustness | Vacuous cross-seed test removed; unmet old criterion recorded; replacement ε-perturbation protocol actually changes input and has predeclared acceptance limits. | Inputs/outputs and assertion evidence from a deliberately failing perturbation. | **Fail:** vacuous test remains. |
| G4 — transcoder migration | Flagship default and real end-to-end load path resolve the deployed Gemma-2-2B layer-12, width-16k, `average_l0_6` dictionary and preserve local feature identity. | Source-set identity record, input hash, real-derived loader/pipeline test. | **Fail:** default config remains `l0_variant=604`; tests mock HF dispatch; no real l0=6 end-to-end attestation. |
| G5 — UMAP migration | Canonical CPU image installs from committed lock without re-lock or parallel pip dependency graph; target generation/validation succeeds. | Image digest, installed-package report, lock hash, output checksums/invariants. | **Fail:** both Dockerfiles bypass lock; full suite cannot collect in this orb because `hdbscan` is absent. |
| G6 — SAE resume smoke | SAE path still loads a real-derived, semantics-free fixture through the same shared contracts at the freeze SHA. | Test evidence and fixture provenance/hash. | **Open:** PR #9 only skips when real tree is absent; A.5b#4 remains. |
| G7 — 2× execution | Two independently provisioned fresh environments reproduce G1-G6 on the same G0 basis. | Two machine-attested run records. | **Fail:** no test CI and no two clean environment reproductions. |
| G8 — static review | CodeRabbit/manual review has no unresolved correctness finding on the exact SHA. | Review permalink + resolution map. | Separate from G7; never counts as an execution. |

No gate run may write production files under `frontend/public/data/` without explicit approval. Test outputs belong in disposable paths. Visual verification of any eventual generated atlas remains user-owned.

### 3.3 Precise definition of “verified 2×”

Two passes means:

1. two independently provisioned clean containers/runners, not two invocations in one mutable environment;
2. identical code SHA, lock hash, canonical image digest, input hashes, and command set;
3. no reuse of generated outputs or dependency caches as test inputs;
4. each pass executes the affected positive paths;
5. the gate suite includes negative/perturbation tests that demonstrate failure sensitivity;
6. both records preserve exit status and output hashes/invariants.

CodeRabbit is an additional independent **review**, not an independent **run**. Local execution plus CodeRabbit is one run plus one static review, not 2× verification.

Minimal CI would provide useful machine attestation, but GitHub Actions is not the only valid implementation. Two clean reproducible runners can satisfy G7 if their environment identity and output evidence are durable. What is not acceptable is substituting a prose review for execution.

### 3.4 Scope deterministic claims narrowly

“Any nondeterminism is fail” is too broad. Require bit-exact output only inside the canonical CPU image and architecture where it is an intended contract. Across architectures/backends, use pre-registered numeric tolerances and topology/quality invariants. Record all scopes explicitly.

The reported ~2,898 stable math-lane executions show repeatability of those checks, not correctness of a vacuous criterion. Repetition cannot rescue a test whose input variation has been deleted.

### 3.5 Checkpoint mechanism: tag, do not park a branch

A long-lived `sae/parked-v6.2` branch will diverge from shared geometry fixes and accumulate rebase risk. Prefer:

1. finish G0-G8;
2. preserve the exact state with an immutable annotated tag;
3. keep the resumability record current on the main line/private plan record;
4. create an SAE branch from the tag only when work resumes.

Tag creation/push is shared Git state and requires explicit approval when the gate is actually ready.

H2 adds little: `poetry.lock` is already the environment lock. A package list and `poetry check --lock` transcript do not prove which environment executed. Record the image digest, Python/platform, lock and pyproject hashes, installed-distribution report, inputs, commands, and outputs instead.

H3 should not commit raw bulky transcripts. Logs can expose paths, environment details, fetched semantics, or other sensitive data. Store scrubbed summaries and checksums; keep any full private artifacts in an approved private store.

### 3.6 Two-tier resume record

There is a tension between reproducibility and repository privacy: `img/**` is gitignored, so a private `RESUME-SAE.md` does not travel with a clean public clone.

Recommended split:

- **Public-safe machine manifest, only if approved:** hashes, source identifiers, commands, image digest, schema versions, test names, and no semantic labels or private strategy.
- **Private plan record:** decision rationale, competitive strategy, owners, unresolved paper claims, and links to private evidence under `img/correction/v6_plans/`/the approved memory system.

The manifest must state which shared fixes landed after the tag and which must be replayed when a branch is created.

### B.5 `audit/` is isolated, but not yet claim-grade

The audit lane has sound foundations:

- gross input dimensionality and feature-count mismatches fail closed;

Those strengths do not make its scientific decision trustworthy. Current blocking defects are:

| Severity | Defect | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | `load_vgt()` reads `localDimensions` without `dimMethod`; current producer writes PR there. | The “VGT concordance” gate compares Euclidicity to PR while reporting VGT. |
| **High** | A constant input can produce Pearson `NaN`; `NaN < 0.3` is false. | The hard gate can fail open and write a memo containing `nan`. |
| **High** | Two finite pairs are enough and scored coverage is warning-only; the decision denominator excludes unscored features. | A nominal whole-dictionary decision can rest on two points, and sparse scales can bias the decision fraction. |
| **High** | The public `seed` is discarded and cross-seed equality is then cited as robustness. | The robustness test is true by construction and supplies no perturbation evidence. |
| **High** | The implementation is a custom adaptation: local-PCA cutoff, dimension cap, annulus fallback, four model draws, H0 log-profile distance, trimming, denoising, gains, and decision thresholds are local choices. | Calling it a port of the paper is too strong until the reference method and benchmarks are reproduced. |
| **High** | No held-out controls, sensitivity/null calibration, uncertainty analysis, or production cosine-mode tests exist. | Passing the three development controls can reflect tuning rather than general validity. |
| **High** | The memo omits method/schema version, SHA/dirty state, hashes, dictionary identity/revision, feature-order identity, complete constants, scale-wise coverage, environment/platform, output hashes, and a completion marker. | A future reader cannot establish what ran or whether a memo belongs to its arrays. |
| **Medium** | `run-id` accepts traversal/absolute paths; existing output is silently overwritten; array and memo writes are non-atomic. A failed rerun can leave an old memo. | Results may escape the intended directory or appear current when they are stale/mixed. |
| **Medium** | Input validation omits non-finite vectors, zero vectors in cosine mode, duplicate/unsorted/non-finite radii, and method/schema validation. Count equality does not prove feature order. | Wrong inputs can pass structural checks or be compared positionally to another dictionary/order. |
| **Medium** | `load_vgt()` parses the entire potentially semantic-bearing dataset instead of a minimal geometry sidecar. | The audit unnecessarily crosses the semantic-data boundary even though it reads one numeric field. |
